How it works

From request to donation, in four steps.

01
Hospital posts a request
Blood group, units, and urgency shared instantly with our matching engine.
02
Eligible donors matched
Filtered by blood compatibility, city, availability, and cooldown status.
03
Donor accepts & navigates
One tap opens live directions straight to the hospital.
04
Verified & rewarded
Hospital scans a QR to confirm the donation; points and cooldown apply automatically.
